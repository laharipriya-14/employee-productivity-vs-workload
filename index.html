⚡ WorkPulse AI — Productivity vs Workload Intelligence

AI agent that detects operational bottlenecks and recommends workload interventions · Assumed data: 6 departments, 48 employees, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026

📌 What the problem statement means

Management sees a paradox: employees are working longer hours, yet output per person is falling. More hours are not producing more results, which usually points to hidden causes: burnout, overloaded teams, poor task distribution, low engagement and rising absenteeism.

As a data analyst you must build an AI-driven system that combines six data sources, finds where work is getting stuck (bottlenecks), who is at risk, and what to change (workload interventions).

🎯 Inputs → Outputs

Inputs: workload (hours), overtime, absenteeism, task completion, engagement, performance.

Outputs: bottleneck score per department, burnout risk per employee, root-cause explanation, ranked interventions, what-if simulation of their impact.

🧠 Solution approach

1
Collect and clean HR, timesheet and task-tool data
2
Engineer features: overtime %, absence rate, completion rate, engagement trend
3
Score bottlenecks: weighted index of overtime, absence, low completion, low engagement
4
Score burnout risk per employee, find correlations
5
AI agent explains causes and recommends interventions
6
Simulator estimates the effect before management acts

🧮 Bottleneck formula (assumed)

Score = 30% Overtime + 20% Absenteeism + 25% (100 − Task completion) + 25% (100 − Engagement), each scaled 0–100. Score ≥ 55 = Critical, 40–55 = Watch, below 40 = Healthy.

Burnout risk = 35% hours above 40, 25% overtime, 20% absence days, 20% low engagement.
